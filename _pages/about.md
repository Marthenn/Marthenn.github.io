## About
Hi! I'm **Marthen** (Bintang Dwi Marthen), an incoming **PhD student in Computer Science** at [the Harvard John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences (SEAS)](https://www.seas.harvard.edu/), starting **Fall 2026**, where I will be advised by [Prof. Juncheng Yang](https://junchengyang.com/).

I work broadly on **data systems**, with a focus on the **efficiency**, **performance**, **reliability**, and **sustainability** of large-scale data systems — spanning **caching algorithms**, **storage systems**, **cloud and serverless computing**, and **systems for ML**.

I'm broadly interested in building smarter and more efficient infrastructure for **data-intensive** and **machine learning workloads**.
