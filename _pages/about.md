## About
Hi! I'm **Marthen** (Bintang Dwi Marthen), a **PhD student in Computer Science** at [the Harvard John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences (SEAS)](https://www.seas.harvard.edu/), advised by [Prof. Juncheng Yang](https://junchengyang.com/).

I build practical systems that make **data-intensive** and **AI workloads** more efficient. My current interests span **caching and storage systems**, **learning-augmented systems**, **cloud and serverless computing**, and **systems for agentic AI and high-performance computing (HPC)**. I am especially interested in simple, deployable mechanisms that improve performance, robustness, and resource efficiency at scale.

Before Harvard, I completed fast-track bachelor's and master's degrees in Computer Science at the [Bandung Institute of Technology (ITB)](https://www.itb.ac.id/), graduating summa cum laude from the bachelor's program. My [master's thesis](https://digilib.itb.ac.id/gdl/view_data/otomatisasi-manajemen-katalog-citra-dasar-kontainer-berbasis-telemetri-beban-kerja-untuk-mereduksi-latensi-cold-start-23525053) explored workload-telemetry-driven management of container base images to reduce cold-start latency in serverless platforms.
